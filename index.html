Bio

I'm Pei Yuan (commonly known as Peipei). I'm a cognitive scientist and a documentary director/storyteller.

For the science part, I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow in the Psychology Department at NYU (Ph.D. in neuroscience from NYU). I am interested in decision-making, affect, and mental health.

For the storytelling part, I'm a documentary director and a nonfiction writer with a deep passion for telling the stories of people who are marginalized. I am deeply interested in people's life experiences, and my documentary work is character-driven. I interview people and host a podcast. I do nonfiction writing about people's lives, and also my own life.

Before coming to NYU to study human behavior at age 26, I was studying particle behavior by majoring in Physics. I earned my B.A. in Physics (with a minor in Psychology) in Nanjing and began a Ph.D. program in theoretical physics in Beijing, which I left to transition to neuroscience/psychology.
